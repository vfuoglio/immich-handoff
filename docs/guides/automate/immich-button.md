# Handing over from inside Immich

If your administrator has put the Handoff button in Immich's pages ([Make Handoff easy to find](../../admin/find-handoff.md#hand-over-from-inside-immich)), you can hand photos over without tags: open a photo, or select several, and press **Hand over...** in the toolbar (or choose **Hand over...** in the three-dot menu). Pick the service; Immich's page then says how many photos were handed over, with **Undo** for a moment. The list shows only services you send to from this page, says whether your original stays or goes to your trash, and warns when photos will wait (a pause, a daily limit). The service you used last is first in the list; **Forget my choice** clears it. A service whose owner decides is listed under **Ask to join**; pressing it asks, and the list then says **Waiting for approval**. Only your own photos can be handed over, up to 200 at once. It does the same as putting the tag on, so everything above about limits, quiet hours and taking back applies.

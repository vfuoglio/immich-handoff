# Pool a trip: everybody gets everybody's photos

For a holiday with friends who all have an account on your Immich server. Each person keeps taking photos with their own phone; a photo any of you tags goes to everyone else.

1. **Offer an event pool.** On **Services**, make a service and tick **Event pool**. Optionally give it an end (1 to 366 days). Choose how people join: by invitation, with your approval, or open to anyone on the server.
2. **Invite the others** with the link or QR code. Each person joins from the page.
3. **Tag a photo** with the service's tag in Immich, or press **Hand over...** if your administrator [added the button](../find-handoff.md#hand-over-from-inside-immich). Everyone else gets a copy tagged `from/<your name>`, so they can browse by photographer.
4. **End it** when the trip is over, from the pool's row. What was handed over stays.

**What you see:** each person's own originals stay where they are, a photo is never sent back to its sender, and people get what is shared *from the moment they join*.

**Worth knowing:** joining a pool lets everyone in it have your tagged photos. Offer it by invitation or with approval if you want to choose who is in. Details: [Event pool](../using.md#event-pool-everybody-gets-everybodys-photos) and [Security](../security.md#event-pools).

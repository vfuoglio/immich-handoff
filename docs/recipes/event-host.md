# Collect an event's photos from guests who have no Immich

For a wedding, a birthday, a club night. You run Immich and have [installed Handoff](../install.md). Your guests need only a phone and a browser.

1. **Make the box.** Open the page, go to **Services**, then **Make a drop box**. Name it after the event, write a short message for guests, and set when it opens and closes (for example Saturday 14:00 to Sunday 12:00).
2. **Choose what guests may do.** Tick **I look at every file before it is kept** if you want to approve photos first, and **Guests may look at the photos and heart them** for a gallery. Leave the rest as it is; every setting is [explained here](../using.md#drop-boxes-photos-from-people-who-do-not-have-immich).
3. **Share the link.** Copy the link or message, or show the **QR code** on a card or a screen. Guests open it, choose photos and send them. They never see your library.
4. **Watch them arrive.** Photos land in your library tagged `inbox/<name of the box>`. Start a [wall](../using.md#the-wall) on a TV for the room to see them come in.
5. **Afterwards.** Make a [best-of](../using.md#a-best-of) of the photos guests loved, download everything as a ZIP, or [hand the photos on](../using.md#handing-the-photos-on) to someone's library.

**What you see:** the photos in your Immich, the counts and the log on the page, and, if you chose to approve, a card on the Overview waiting for your answer.

**Worth knowing:** the same photo sent twice is stored once; a file is checked by its content, not its name; a password can protect the upload or the whole page. See [Security](../security.md#drop-boxes) for what a guest can and cannot do.

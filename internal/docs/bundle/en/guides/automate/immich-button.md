# Handing over from inside Immich

A **Hand over...** button in Immich's own pages hands photos over without tags: open a photo, or select several, and press it.

![Immich's selection bar with the Hand over button and the list of services](../../images/immich-button-selection-list.jpg)

## Use it

If your administrator has put the Handoff button in Immich's pages ([Make Handoff easy to find](../../admin/find-handoff.md#hand-over-from-inside-immich)), open a photo, or select several, and press **Hand over...** in the toolbar (or choose **Hand over...** in the three-dot menu). Pick the service; Immich's page then says how many photos were handed over, with **Undo** for a moment.

- The list shows only services you send to from this page, says whether your original stays or goes to your trash, and warns when photos will wait (a pause, a daily limit).
- The service you used last is first in the list; **Forget my choice** clears it.
- A service whose owner decides is listed under **Ask to join**; pressing it asks, and the list then says **Waiting for approval**.
- Only your own photos can be handed over, up to 200 at once.

It does the same as putting the tag on, so everything about limits, quiet hours and taking back applies: [Control what is handed over](controls.md).

## If something is off

- **There is no button.** Your administrator has not put it in place, or you are in the phone app (it works in a browser): [Make Handoff easy to find](../../admin/find-handoff.md).
- **A service is missing from the list.** It lists only services you send to from this page.
